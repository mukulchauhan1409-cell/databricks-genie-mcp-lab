# Sample Business Questions

These questions are intended for a small customer and order dataset.

## Revenue

1. What is the total revenue?
2. What is the total revenue by region?
3. Which customer generated the most revenue?

## Orders

4. What are the top five orders by amount?
5. How many orders were placed in each region?
6. What is the average order value?

## Time-based analysis

7. How did revenue change by day?
8. What was the highest-revenue day?
